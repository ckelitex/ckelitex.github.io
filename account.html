Email subscriptions

CKEliteX collects your email address when you voluntarily subscribe to CKEliteX Insiders. Your email is used to deliver gaming content, early-access clips, announcements and community updates based on your consent.

Email subscriptions are managed through Kit, a third-party email marketing platform. Kit processes subscriber information to provide email delivery and subscription management. Information may be processed outside the European Economic Area. See the Kit Privacy Policy for more information.

Insider recognition

This website uses local browser storage to remember visitors who have reached the Insider welcome page. No email address or password is stored by this feature. Browser recognition is not secure account authentication. Clearing your website data removes this preference. This does not unsubscribe you from CKEliteX emails.

Gaming accounts

Supabase provides account authentication and stores your account email, profile, points, streak and last reward claim. Browser storage maintains your login session. Gaming accounts are separate from optional newsletter subscriptions. Contact us to request account deletion or access to your data. See Supabase privacy information.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to measure website usage. Analytics is optional and is not loaded until you accept analytics cookies. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

You can withdraw or change your analytics consent at any time using Cookie Settings below. Google may process information outside the European Economic Area. Read the Google Privacy Policy for more information.

This website includes services provided by Twitch and Kit. These services may process information and use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their privacy policies explain how they handle data.
